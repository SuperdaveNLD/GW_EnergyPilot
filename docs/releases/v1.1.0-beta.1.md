# GW EnergyPilot v1.1.0-beta.1

**Channel:** Beta prerelease

This beta combines every v1.0.1 beta fix with complete managed battery ranges,
the new **Chargegasm** mode, selectable plan-chart ranges and clearer internal
versus external PV flow. It does not change GoodWe EMS mode semantics,
Automatic Control strategy or EMHASS plan ownership.

## Battery profiles

| Mode | Hard range | Comfort zone | Low-SOC cost | High-SOC cost | Power stress | Anti-churn |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mad-Steve | 5–100% | 10–95% | 2% | 5% | 0% | 2.25% |
| Gold Rush | 5–100% | 10–95% | 2% | 12% | 0% | 6% |
| Chargegasm | 8–96% | 13–91% | 2% | 18% | 2% | 6% |
| Balanced | 10–93% | 15–88% | 5% | 25% | 6% | 7% |
| Battery Saver | 10–85% | 20–80% | 10% | 50% | 20% | 9% |

Costs are factors of the dynamic price reference. High-SOC cost is the EMHASS
time-dependent cost per kWh/hour above the comfort-zone ceiling. These values
are optimizer policy, not a battery-specific degradation prediction.

## Dashboard behavior

- The Controller card shows six choices: five managed profiles and Custom.
- Managed profiles show their hard range, comfort zone and main cost factors
  read-only; SOC sliders are not rendered.
- Settings → EMHASS → Battery Saver includes the complete comparison table.
- Custom preserves current values and restores the existing minimum/maximum
  SOC sliders plus the five raw cost inputs.
- While Automatic Control owns the inverter, the existing manual mode grid and
  power slider stay connected but collapse behind a compact ownership summary.
  Selecting manual ownership reveals those same controls again.

## Plan chart ranges

Battery · Plan · Price now remembers a browser-local **12h**, **24h** or **36h**
view:

- 12h follows six hours before through six hours after now;
- 24h is the current Home Assistant-local calendar day;
- 36h runs from today 00:00 through tomorrow 12:00.

The backend supplies timezone- and DST-aware boundaries. Switching range only
filters the one cached dataset and does not start another Recorder query. The
canonical chart card, header and controls remain connected during refresh.

## Internal and external PV flow

The small PV group retains one combined total while showing at most two source
nodes: internal GoodWe/ETA DC PV and one aggregate of up to four configured
external AC/PCC PV entities. Internal PV ends at the battery-side branch;
external AC-coupled PV ends at the shared PCC side. This split is display-only
and does not infer source attribution or change `pv_generation_power`, EMHASS,
Automatic Control, accounting or GoodWe writes.

## GoodWe/EMHASS transaction

Selecting a managed profile:

1. requires a readable current GoodWe on-grid minimum;
2. writes the profile minimum to verified register `45356` and checks read-back;
3. persists managed ownership and applies all ten owned EMHASS fields;
4. clamps runtime `soc_final`, optimizes and publishes the fresh plan;
5. restores the previous GoodWe minimum, option, runtime state and owned EMHASS
   values if the first complete cycle fails.

Direct writes to either SOC NumberEntity are rejected while a managed profile
is active. Selecting Custom releases that ownership without resetting values.
An existing v1.0 managed profile is not allowed to cause a hardware write on
upgrade alone; select it again to activate its new managed range.

## Rationale and limitations

Published LFP/graphite studies support treating average SOC, SOC range,
temperature, charge/discharge rate and throughput as separate aging factors.
EnergyPilot can influence SOC dwell, power and marginal cycling but does not
measure cell temperature or implement a battery-specific lifetime model. See
`docs/BATTERY_SAVER.md` for sources, interpretation and maintenance caveats.

## Compatibility

- EMHASS remains an external prerequisite and canonical plan owner.
- Entity unique IDs, device identity, persistent Stores, registers and EMS
  setpoint-before-mode order are unchanged.
- Managed profiles still support exactly one EMHASS battery model; Custom can
  release ownership for multi-battery installations without rewriting values.
- Desktop Chromium, iPad WebKit touch and iPhone WebKit touch regressions cover
  range selection, stable manual controls and the two PV routes.
