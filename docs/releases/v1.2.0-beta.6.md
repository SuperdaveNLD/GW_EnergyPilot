# GW EnergyPilot v1.2.0-beta.6

**Channel:** Beta prerelease

This prerelease improves the remaining compact Battery · Plan · Price and
execution-history controls in Home Assistant Companion. It retains beta.5's
bounded iOS missing-click recovery and does not change GoodWe, EMS, EMHASS or
Battery Saver semantics.

## Larger mobile targets

On coarse-pointer or narrow displays, the following native controls now have a
real minimum 44 × 44 CSS-pixel button box:

- Battery · Plan · Price `S/M/L` size segments;
- `12h/24h/36h` range segments;
- chart expand and footer actions;
- full 48-hour execution-history open and close controls.

The desktop presentation remains compact. On an iPhone-sized viewport the two
segmented groups wrap normally without horizontal card overflow.

## Interaction behavior

The controls retain their existing click handlers and browser-local preference
storage. When Companion omits native click after a valid primary touch,
beta.5's root-scoped adapter still waits 120 ms and invokes only that same
element's `.click()` path. Movement beyond 12 px, pointer cancellation,
disabled/disconnected targets and secondary touches remain rejected; one late
physical click is deduplicated.

## Validation

The desktop Chromium, iPad WebKit touch and iPhone WebKit touch matrix now
removes native click from the real size, range and history controls. It proves:

- every size and range selection changes exactly once and persists;
- full history opens once and its close action survives removing its own node;
- all named touch targets reach at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels on touch profiles;
- the enlarged groups do not overflow the card;
- no page or JavaScript errors occur.

Physical Home Assistant Companion verification remains required. After
upgrade, tap each size/range segment once, open and close full history, and
start one vertical scroll on the same controls to confirm it causes no action.

The complete active frontend graph uses cache boundary
`1.2.0-beta.6-chart-touch1`.
