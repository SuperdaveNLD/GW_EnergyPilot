# GW EnergyPilot v1.1.0

**Channel:** Stable

GW EnergyPilot v1.1.0 promotes the validated `v1.1.0-beta.1` candidate to the
production channel. Runtime, optimizer and GoodWe control behavior are
unchanged from that approved candidate; only the stable version presentation,
cache boundary and release documentation differ.

## Battery profiles

| Mode | Hard range | Comfort zone | Low-SOC cost | High-SOC cost | Power stress | Anti-churn |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mad-Steve | 5–100% | 10–95% | 2% | 5% | 0% | 2.25% |
| Gold Rush | 5–100% | 10–95% | 2% | 12% | 0% | 6% |
| Chargegasm | 8–96% | 13–91% | 2% | 18% | 2% | 6% |
| Balanced | 10–93% | 15–88% | 5% | 25% | 6% | 7% |
| Battery Saver | 10–85% | 20–80% | 10% | 50% | 20% | 9% |

Costs are factors of the dynamic price reference. High-SOC cost is the EMHASS
time-dependent cost per kWh/hour above the comfort-zone ceiling. These values
are transparent optimizer policy, not a battery-specific lifetime prediction.

## Included improvements

- Battery Strategy provides five managed profiles plus Custom. Managed
  profiles display their hard range, comfort zone and cost factors read-only;
  their SOC sliders are hidden until Custom is selected.
- Settings → EMHASS → Battery Saver contains the complete profile comparison.
- Battery · Plan · Price remembers a 12h, 24h or 36h view without starting a
  second Recorder query for each range change.
- The live PV group separates internal GoodWe/ETA DC PV from aggregated
  external AC/PCC PV while retaining one combined PV total.
- Automatic ownership collapses the connected manual controls to a compact
  summary; selecting manual ownership reveals those controls again.
- Every fix from v1.0.1-beta.1 through beta.4 is included, including restart-safe
  scheduling, canonical GoodWe mapping, explicit EV charging detection and
  bounded fresh-plan retries after EV charging stops.

## GoodWe and EMHASS transaction

Selecting a managed profile reads, writes and verifies the profile's GoodWe
minimum SOC, persists managed ownership, applies all ten owned EMHASS fields
and builds a fresh plan as one rollback-safe transaction. A failed first cycle
restores the previous GoodWe minimum, options, runtime state and owned EMHASS
values. Existing v1.0 managed selections still require explicit reselection, so
installing v1.1.0 alone cannot write a different hardware floor.

## Compatibility and installation

- EMHASS remains an external prerequisite and canonical plan owner.
- Entity unique IDs, device identity, persistent Stores, GoodWe registers, EMS
  mode semantics and setpoint-before-mode ordering are unchanged.
- Normal HACS users receive v1.1.0 through the stable channel and restart Home
  Assistant when prompted. The prerelease switch can remain off.
- Desktop Chromium, iPad WebKit touch and iPhone WebKit touch regressions cover
  the promoted dashboard graph and controls.
